## 1. In short

**The App does not collect personal information and does not send anything to our servers.** There is no sign-in, and the App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Your game record stays on your device.

## 2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through the App — no name, email, phone number, device identifiers, advertising ID, location, contacts, or photos.

The following data is created while you play and is **stored only on your device**. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see or retrieve it.

| Stored on your device | Why |
|---|---|
| Coordinates and timing of the strokes you draw | To draw your Mongmangi's body and growth |
| Your sleep and morning times, and when your Mongmangi slept and woke | To work out each "day" (one sleep) |
| Game progress (temperament, stroke count, morning card history) | So you can continue where you left off |
| Settings (notifications, sound, reduced motion, etc.) | To keep your preferences |

- Device tilt is used in the moment to make your Mongmangi glance around. It is not stored.

## 3. Device backup and export

- If you have device backup turned on (iCloud Backup on iOS, Auto Backup on Android), your game record may be included in **your own Apple or Google account backup**. That backup is handled under Apple's or Google's terms; we have no access to it. [To verify in build: backup inclusion (D-06)]
- A save export code is something you copy and keep yourself. It is not sent to us.

## 4. Notifications

- The App uses **local notifications scheduled on your device only** (for example, "Last night's dream has developed"). We do not collect push tokens or device information.
- You choose whether to allow notifications when first asked, and you can turn them off anytime in your device settings.

## 5. Saving and sharing morning cards

- A morning card is saved or shared **only when you tap to do so**. Sharing goes through your device's standard share sheet to the app you choose; we do not receive it.
- Saving a card to your photo library may prompt for photo access (add-only). The App does not read your existing photos. [To verify in build: save method and permission scope]

## 6. Sharing, processors, and international transfers

Because we collect no personal information, we do not share it with third parties, use processors for it, or transfer it abroad.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- We hold no personal information.
- Your on-device game record is **deleted when you uninstall the App**. Copies in your device backup can be removed from your Apple or Google account's backup settings.

## 8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. Since we hold none, you can delete your game record immediately by uninstalling the App. For any question, email us at the address below.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If this ever changes, we will put a parental consent process in place first and update this policy.

## 10. Tracking

The App uses no cookies or similar tracking technologies, and does not link data with other companies' apps or websites to track you.

## 11. Security

We do not collect or store personal information. The App keeps your game record in app-private storage (an area the operating system protects from other apps).

## 12. If we add crash reporting

If we add crash reporting to help us fix crashes, we will update this policy before that version is released — saying what is sent and where — and let you know in the App. The same applies to any other new data processing.
