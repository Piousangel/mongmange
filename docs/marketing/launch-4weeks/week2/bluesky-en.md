## S3-en. Three objects — 01-19 (Tue) 12:30

- Images: P7 real inkstone, brush and water dropper (4:5) + C13 site capture
- 사실 대조: FACTS en `day` 세 문장(둘째 문장은 300자 상한 때문에 'the word of the day'·'of your writing'을 덜어 줄임 — 뜻은 같음).

```
Each day uses three objects.

Grind ink on the inkstone. How long you grind sets how much ink there is and how dark it is.
Write one word with the brush. The longest stroke stays on its body.
Set it down at the water dropper. The water loosens the word into that night's dream.

#gamedev
```
